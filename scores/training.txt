leading-none font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.16em] text-foreground/85">FAQ</div></div><div data-slot="card-content" class="divide-y divide-border/60 px-4 py-0"><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">How is the baseline curve calculated?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>For each content, we take every ranked character's CP and their score from the latest snapshot. On a log–log scale, score rises with CP along a near-straight line, so we fit a single line through<!-- --> <strong>all classes pooled together</strong>. That line is the dashed “baseline” on the chart — the score a typical player puts up at a given CP.</p><p class="mt-2">The fit uses the <strong>median</strong>, not the average, so a handful of outliers and players who perform very badly in the content don't drag the line up or down. Each class's bar below the chart is then its<!-- --> <strong>median gap</strong> from that shared baseline: positive means the class typically out-scores the field at equal CP, negative means it lands below.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">Why are 4th Job and 3rd Job shown separately?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>Score-per-CP scales differently for 4th job and 3rd job classes, so mixing them would distort the curve. We split players into two cohorts —<!-- --> <strong>4th Job (Lv 100+)</strong> and <strong>3rd Job (Lv 60–99)</strong> — and fit a separate baseline for each. Looking up a character automatically switches to the cohort matching their level.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">Why don't all players show up as dots on the chart?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>The scatter only plots a <strong>sample</strong> of each class — a handful of players spread evenly across the CP range — so the chart stays readable and fast. The dots are illustration only. The baseline curve and every class's gap are computed from the <strong>full population</strong> of ranked players, not the sample you see.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">Why are some classes greyed out?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>A class needs at least <strong>100 ranked players</strong> in that mode before its median is somewhat trustworthy. Below that it's still listed but marked<!-- --> <strong>not enough data</strong> instead of a number we can't stand behind, and it's left off the scatter dots. Either way it quietly counts toward the pooled baseline curve. As more players rank up, the class fills in automatically.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">What's the difference between “vs all classes” and “vs my class”?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>When you look up a character, the card shows two numbers:</p><ul class="mt-2 list-disc space-y-1 pl-5"><li><strong>vs all classes at this CP</strong> — how the score compares to the shared baseline (every class pooled) at that CP.</li><li><strong>vs your class at this CP</strong> — how it compares to a<!-- --> <em>typical player of the same class</em> at that CP, after adjusting for the class's own median gap.</li></ul><p class="mt-2">So a class that naturally sits below the baseline can still be well above average <em>for its class</em> — the second number is the fairer like-for-like read.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">Which score and CP does a lookup show?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>The card shows your <strong>best recorded score</strong> for that mode, paired with the <strong>CP you had when you set it</strong> — not your current CP. Pinning the score to the CP it was achieved at keeps the comparison honest: if you've grown your CP since, that older score isn't judged against your higher CP. The<!-- --> <strong>Date</strong> is the snapshot that best score came from.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">Why do I perform below the curve as a top player?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>At the very high end of CP there are only a handful of players, so the baseline has very little data to fit against up there. The line is dominated by the <strong>dense middle of the population</strong>, where most players sit, and gets extrapolated outward to the extremes. It's a limitation of the sparse data up there  — the fit is most accurate for the majority of players in the middle of the range. As we get more data at these high CPs, we can revisit if this model needs to be updated.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">Why can't I find my character?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>Due to scraping limitations, we can't fetch every character's data —<!-- --> <!-- -->only <strong>top characters</strong> are likely to be captured. If your character doesn't turn up in a lookup, it most likely hasn't been scraped yet. As coverage expands, more characters fill in automatically.</p></div></details><details class="group"><summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 text-sm font-medium transition-colors hover:text-foreground/80 [&amp;::-webkit-details-marker]:hidden">How current is the data?</summary><div class="pb-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground"><p>Everything comes from the most recent snapshot. The<!-- --> <strong>“Last updated”</strong> date next to each chart shows when that snapshot was taken. Content based on recurring seasons — Guild Conquest and Training Ground — uses the final scores from the latest completed season, while World Boss reflects the latest available results, so the dates can differ.</p></div></details></div></div></div><!--$--><!--/$--><!--/$--><!--/$--></div></main><footer class="mt-12 flex flex-col items-center gap-4 border-t border-border/50 pt-4 pb-4"><button data-slot="button" data-variant="outline" data-size="icon-sm" class="inline-flex items-center justify-center gap-2 whitespace-nowrap rounded-md text-sm font-medium transition-[color,background-color,border-color,box-shadow,opacity] disabled:pointer-events-none disabled:opacity-50 [&amp;_svg]:pointer-events-none [&amp;_svg:not([class*='size-'])]:size-4 shrink-0 [&amp;_svg]:shrink-0 outline-none focus-visible:border-ring focus-visible:ring-ring/50 focus-visible:ring-[3px] aria-invalid:ring-destructive/20 dark:aria-invalid:ring-destructive/40 aria-invalid:border-destructive border bg-background shadow-xs hover:bg-accent hover:text-accent-foreground dark:bg-input/30 dark:border-input dark:hover:bg-input/50 size-8" type="button" aria-label="Switch to dark mode" aria-pressed="false" title="Switch to dark mode" fdprocessedid="wf8j6"><span class="flex items-center justify-center" style="opacity: 1;
